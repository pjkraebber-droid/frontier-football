**Vermont Green FC Makes History — Back-to-Back USL2 Champions**

Vermont Green FC closed the books on the 2026 USL League Two season in the most emphatic way possible. 
Vermont Green FC defeated Flint City Bucks 2–1 in the 2026 USL League Two Final on Saturday night in Flint, Michigan, and were crowned Undefeated National Champions for the second straight season.
 The final was a composed performance from a Burlington side that had to win it away, in front of over 6,000 opposing fans.


The two finalists went into halftime at 0–0, before Vermont struck first when winger Diego Rosas crossed a ball in the 62nd minute that resulted in an own goal. Vermont's lead was short-lived, as Bucks player Matthew Paiva scored on a header to even the game at 1–1 just two minutes later.
 
Forward David Ajagbe came on as a substitute in the 73rd minute and immediately chased down a pass. Flint City goalkeeper — and MLS veteran — Steve Clark pulled him down in the box, resulting in a penalty. Attacking midfielder Ryan Zellefrow stepped to the spot and slotted home the winner; he was named 2026 USL2 Final MVP.


The broader context makes this result remarkable. 
The Boys in Green concluded the 2026 USL2 season with an 18W–1D–0L record, scoring 85 goals, and have now gone 42 matches unbeaten in all competitions, a run stretching back to July 21, 2024.
 
Vermont Green became the first club to win back-to-back USL2 championships since the Cape Cod Crusaders in 2002–2003.
 Coach Chris Taylor's operation in Burlington has set a standard that, at this point in the summer, no one in the tier has come close to matching.

### 🏟️ CLUB NEWS

**Sacramento Republic FC** remain the most active infrastructure story in the division. 
Heavy equipment operators are at work in Sacramento's historic Railyards district, laying the groundwork for Republic Stadium — a landmark, soccer-specific venue that will be the largest professional sports and entertainment arena in the region, built on a 31-acre parcel of ancestral lands of the Wilton Rancheria tribal nation.
 
The Wilton Rancheria and Republic FC added 8,000 more seats to the project; originally slated for 12,000, the new stadium will now have a capacity of more than 20,000, shifting from a phased expansion model to a full-scale buildout from day one.
 
Groundbreaking took place in August 2025; Republic FC is now aiming for a 2028 opening.
