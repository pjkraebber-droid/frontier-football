### 📌 FRONTIER NOTE — USL Premier & Pro/Rel


The United Soccer League announced that its new Division One setup will be called "USL Premier," with a targeted start date of 2028. The new top division will sit atop a three-tiered structure that also includes the pre-existing USL Championship and USL League One.



USL Premier (Division One) will operate as a single national table with a long-term target of 20 clubs; USL Championship (Division Two) will also operate as a single national table with 20 clubs; and USL League One (Division Three) will continue to grow nationally. As this structure takes shape, the USL will introduce America's first promotion and relegation system — a connected, merit-based professional pathway.


The CBA and television rights remain the two variables that could delay or modify the 2028 launch. 
Television rights and the new CBA are going to be key factors in getting this launched.
 For clubs currently in League One with real stadium infrastructure — Fort Wayne, Athletic Club Boise, One Knoxville — the pathway just acquired stakes it never had before.
