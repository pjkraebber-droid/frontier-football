### 🏟️ CLUB NEWS

**USL League Two | Flint City Bucks vs. Vermont Green FC — Final Preview**


The Flint City Bucks will host Vermont Green FC in the 2026 USL League Two Final on Saturday, August 1 from Atwood Stadium in Flint, Michigan.
 The stakes are straightforward: 
Flint looks to extend its record for most League Two titles with its fifth, while Vermont aims to become the first repeat champion in 23 years.


Vermont's path here has been dominant. 
Unlike many top American sports leagues that determine playoff hosts based on seeding, USL2 selects playoff hosts through a bidding process. Despite the Green finishing the regular season tied for the highest point total across the league, the club has only hosted one weekend of playoff games.
 That's the context for the 7-1 road semifinal result — this team wins away from home without apology.


The Bucks are perhaps the most successful amateur soccer club in the United States, with four USL2 National Championships in 2006, 2013, 2016, and 2019. In addition to their national titles, they have won their division 18 times, their conference 11 times, and qualified for the U.S. Open Cup 20 times.
