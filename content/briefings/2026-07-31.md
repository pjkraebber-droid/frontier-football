## 📋 TOP STORY — USL2 FINAL EVE: FLINT CITY VS. VERMONT GREEN, HISTORY ON THE LINE

Tomorrow night's USL League Two National Final is the most narratively loaded match this league has staged in years, and it earns top billing here.


The Flint City Bucks will host Vermont Green FC in the 2026 USL League Two Final on Saturday, August 1 from Atwood Stadium in Flint, Michigan.
 
Flint looks to extend its record for most League Two titles with its fifth, while Vermont aims to become the first repeat champion in 23 years.


Vermont got here the convincing way. 
The Green will arrive in the USL2 National Final with an undefeated record through the regular season and playoffs, after traveling to Texas to defeat Laredo Heat SC 7-1 in the league semifinal on Sunday.
 
The defending League Two champion flexed its muscles in the second half, scoring five goals — including the second two of Ryan Zellefrow's hat trick — to claim a 7-1 victory.
 
Head Coach Chris Taylor's unbeaten streak now reaches 41 matches — he has not recorded a loss as gaffer of the Green.


Flint's route was tighter. 
Just as the semifinal against Ventura County Fusion looked destined for extra time, Richard Ludwig found Oliver Moller-Jensen with room in the box, and the Bucks' leading scorer converted the opportunity, sending Atwood Stadium into a frenzy and Flint to its seventh League Two Final.
 
The Bucks eliminated FC Buffalo, Steel City FC, Peoria City, and Ventura County Fusion in that order during their two-week march to the title tilt.



The title holders take on the four-time champions in what is expected to be the 31-year-old league's most-watched final ever.
 Kickoff at Atwood Stadium is 7:30 PM ET.
